Embedded Austria runs regular evening meetups in Vienna for people who build embedded
systems for a living. Each event is three or four talks from working engineers, followed
by open networking. No vendor pitches, no marketing decks.

Past events have covered embedded Linux and Yocto, real-time operating systems,
containers on edge devices, the Cyber Resilience Act and NIS2, functional safety, and AI
on embedded hardware.

Registration goes through Eventbrite. Ticket prices are listed with each event.
